# Nesil: Privacy policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026 · [Türkçe](gizlilik.html)

Nesil is a life simulation game you play on your phone. This page explains what data is kept where, and where it goes.

## In short

- You don't need an account. We don't ask for your name, e-mail or phone number.
- Your save, the answers you write and your lives' journals stay **only on your phone**. We have no server; this information is not sent to us or anyone else.
- The game has optional **rewarded ads**. Ads are served by Google AdMob, and Google collects some device and usage data (see below).
- If you buy the full version, payment goes through Apple; your payment details never reach us.

## Data that stays on your phone

The game itself stores the following only on your device, in the app's own folder:

- The life you're playing, your family tree, your family book and your life archive.
- The answers you write to open-ended questions.
- Your settings (appearance, sound, music, question frequency) and your new-life allowance.

None of this is sent to any server. Deleting the app deletes all of it. Settings > "Reset all data" deletes your lives, family tree, family book, archive and life allowance; your appearance, sound and question-frequency settings are kept. If your phone's own backup (for example iCloud Backup) is on, these files may be included in it; that is a setting between you and Apple.

If you choose to share a life, the share card is created as an image on your phone and sent only to the app you pick after tapping "Share". The card shows the character's name, country, years of birth and death and, if you chose one, an answer you wrote.

The privacy, terms and data-source links in Settings open in your browser; those sites (GitHub Pages and the source institutions) handle your visit under their own policies.

## Ads: Google AdMob

You can watch a short ad to earn a new life. Watching an ad is always your choice; no ad appears on its own in the middle of the game. The full version has no ads at all.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK that serves the ads may collect:

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| IP address, device model, OS version and app information — with every ad request, even if you decline tracking | Serving the ad |
| Advertising identifier (IDFA) — **only if you allow it** | Showing relevant ads, ad measurement |
| Ad interactions (shown, watched) | Ad measurement, fraud prevention |
| Coarse location derived from your IP address (country, city) | Showing ads suited to your region |
| Diagnostics (crashes, performance) | Keeping the ad system working |

- **Tracking permission:** Apple's tracking prompt appears right before the first ad. If you don't allow it, your advertising identifier is not used and you see non-personalized ads. You can change this later in your phone's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
- **European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland:** Google's consent form is shown before ads. You can change your choice in the game's Settings > "Ad privacy options" (this button appears in those regions while ads are on).
- **Ad content:** Ads for adults are turned off; gambling, alcohol, dating and political ads are blocked too.
- Your answers, journal and lives are never sent to the ad network.

How Google uses this data: [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Purchases

The full version is sold through Apple's in-app purchase system. Your payment and account details stay with Apple. We only read from Apple whether the full version has been bought.

## Country data

The country information in the game (life expectancy, fertility, income, schooling, religion shares and so on) comes from open data published by the United Nations, the World Bank, UNDP and Pew Research Center, among others. The full list of sources is in the game under Settings > "Sources and credits". This data is not personal data.

## Children

Nesil is not directed at children under 13. The game deals with heavy topics such as death, illness, poverty and loss; domestic violence, war and addiction come up without graphic detail. We don't collect personal data from anyone; what the ad network collects is described above.

## Your rights

We don't hold any information about you on a server, so there is no record for us to delete on request. You can delete everything in the game by deleting the app or with "Reset all data". For the ad data Google collects, use the options on Google's pages linked above.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
